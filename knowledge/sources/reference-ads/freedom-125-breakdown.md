### 2. MOTION RULES

*   **Coupling is Key:** In over 80% of moving shots, the camera and bike both move. The dominant pattern is **perfect tracking**: the camera moves at the bike's exact speed, keeping the bike locked in the same position within the frame. This makes the bike the stable "hero" while the background streaks by.
*   **Motion is Linear:** Almost all motion paths are straight lines (tracking alongside, tracking back). There is only one arcing shot (the bend) to break the pattern and add dynamism.
*   **No Intra-Shot Location Changes:** The location is consistent within each 2-4 second clip. The scene only changes on the cut.
*   **Background in Motion:** In tracking shots, only the background has visible motion blur. The bike itself remains relatively sharp, emphasizing its stability and the speed of the environment. The wheels have rotational blur.

### 3. EDIT

*   **Pacing:** The edit is fast and rhythmic. The typical shot length is very short, between 2 and 3 seconds.
*   **Cut Timing:** Cuts are timed precisely on the beat of the electronic music track. Major visual reveals (like the word "CHANGE" or "CNG") are punctuated by a cut and a corresponding sound effect/musical hit.
*   **Shot Progression:** There is no linear progression (e.g., WS-MS-CU). Instead, it's a dynamic mix to maintain interest: EWS -> MS -> CU -> MS -> WS -> ECU -> MS. This prevents predictability.
*   **Product Reveal:** The product is revealed gradually. We see it in motion from the start, but specific features (fender, switch, seat, suspension) are highlighted in dedicated close-ups. The final, definitive "hero shot" is a static beauty shot at the end, followed by the price.
*   **Logo/Branding:** The brand logo is present as a watermark in the top-right corner throughout the entire ad.

### 4. SOUND

*   **Music:** A modern, driving, four-on-the-floor electronic track. The energy curve builds steadily, peaks, dips slightly for feature callouts, and then has a final epic swell for the main reveal before resolving.
*   **Effects Design:** Sound effects are crucial and stylized.
    *   **Whooshes:** Used to accentuate cuts and fast camera/bike movements.
    *   **Hits/Risers:** Synced with text reveals and major cuts to add impact.
    *   **Specifics:** A crisp `CLICK` for the fuel switch, a `thump/squish` for the suspension, and a clean, modern engine `hum/rev` rather than a raw, noisy one.
*   **Synchronization:** All sound effects and cuts are tightly synchronized to the music's tempo and key moments.
*   **Mix:** The music forms the bed, but the sound effects (especially the engine, click, and whooshes) are mixed prominently to punch through and direct the viewer's attention. There is no voiceover.

### 5. The 10 Copyable Rules for an AI Pipeline

1.  **Generate 2-4 Second Clips.** All action clips must be short and rhythmic.
2.  **Prioritize Perfect Tracking.** For any "moving alongside" shot, the camera's speed must perfectly match the bike's, keeping the bike's position fixed in the frame.
3.  **Apply Heavy Motion Blur to the Background.** In all tracking shots, the background must be heavily blurred to convey speed. The bike remains sharp. The wheels must have rotational blur.
4.  **Cut on the Beat.** All edits (transitions between clips) must be synchronized to the beat of a 120-128 BPM electronic music track.
5.  **Use Sound Effects for Impact.** Every major cut or text reveal must be accompanied by a synchronized "whoosh" or "hit" sound effect.
6.  **Alternate Shot Sizes.** A sequence must mix Wide, Medium, and Close-Up shots. Do not follow a linear WS -> MS -> CU progression.
7.  **Isolate Features in Close-Ups.** When generating a Close-Up or Extreme Close-Up, it must focus on a single, specific part of the bike (e.g., headlight, switch, shock absorber).
8.  **Render from Low Angles.** Most moving shots should be rendered from eye-level or a slightly low angle to make the motorcycle appear powerful and heroic.
9.  **The "Hero Shot" is Static.** The final product reveal clip must be a slow camera track-in on a *stationary* bike in an iconic, clean location.
10. **One Clip, One Location.** The environment, lighting, and time of day must remain 100% consistent within a single 4-8 second rendered clip.
