# Jay Weigel On The Creative Process of Composing for Commercials
ArtistsHouseMusic · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U1j3mWg__pY

Based on the transcript, here are the concrete, reusable techniques for an AI video-ad pipeline.

### 1. Workflow
*   (Lyria, Gemini) **Analysis-First:** Before generating, perform an "analytical," "dissection process" on the script or brief to extract keywords.
*   (Lyria, Gemini) **Visuals to Emotion to Sound:** Use generated visuals (Gemini) to define an emotion, then use that emotion as a prompt for the sound/music (Lyria).
*   (All tools) **Roadmap & Playground:** Use a separate document ("paper") as a "visual road map" for plotting/planning the ad structure, and the AI tools as the "playground" for execution.

### 2. Keyframe / image prompting
*   (Gemini) **Prompt for Mood/Scope:** Base prompts on a core decision: is the shot intimate or part of a larger scene?
    *   Example: `An intimate, macro photo of a [product]` vs. `A [product] as part of a scene in a [location]`.
*   (Gemini) **Use Abstract Adjectives:** Include emotional or stylistic keywords from your initial analysis, such as `lush background`, `small`, `intimate`.

### 5. Shot design & editing
*   (Editing Software) **Time-Based Plotting:** Analytically plot out musical and visual cues on a timeline, timed "in milliseconds."

### 6. Music & sound
*   (Lyria) **Translate Visuals to Prompts:** Prompt based on the visual style. If the visual is "intimate," use that word in the music prompt.
*   (Lyria) **Prompt Keywords for Style:** Use dichotomies to define the sound: `intimate` vs. `broad`, `hip` vs. `not hip`.
*   (Lyria) **Prompt for Instrumentation & Density:** Be specific about the musical elements and their function.
    *   `solo violin for the main melodic idea`
    *   `string pad to fill the space`
*   (Lyria) **Prompt for Goal:** Explicitly state the musical goal, such as `cover the main melodic idea` or `fill the space up`.

### 7. Common failures and fixes
*   **Failure:** Having no initial musical or visual ideas.
*   **Fix:** Analyze the script/brief for descriptive keywords (`lush`, `intimate`, `small`) to generate a starting point.

### 8. Quotes worth keeping
*   (Gemini/Lyria) "...are we talking here about a lush background are we talking about a small thing an intimate thing..."
*   (Lyria) "...whether it's a solo violin that is going to cover the main melodic idea...or whether it's a string pad that's going to fill the space up..."
*   (Lyria) "...what to you sounds intimate versus sounds broad or sounds hip versus sounds not..."
*   (Workflow) "It is a real dissection process for me... for me it's like mathematics, breaking it down, often plotting it out... in milliseconds."
