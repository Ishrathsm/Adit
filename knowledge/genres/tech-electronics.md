# Genre: Tech and consumer electronics

**Evidence:**
- 76 famous-brand ads in the Pitt dataset (Apple, Samsung, GoPro, Sony, HTC, HP, LG, ASUS, Energizer, Microsoft, Airtel, Havells and others);
- case studies: Apple "Shot on iPhone", Apple "Crazy Ones";
- Apple-style product-film guides.

Full analysis: `../sources/genre-datasets/tech-dataset-analysis.md`.

## What wins

- **The highest-rated genre** (mean effectiveness 4.09). Clarity rules.
- **Choose your battlefield: product or brand.** Don't mix them in 30 seconds.
  - **One feature as the hero:** ASUS battery life ("#NoNeedToRush"); Samsung motion control; Sky "Quicksilver" speed.
  - **Or an identity:** Apple "Crazy Ones" ("because it thinks different"); the iPhone India film about love and memory.
- **Demonstration is the strongest device.** GoPro's "Durability" ad tries to break the camera and needs no voice-over. Samsung's smart-TV ad *is* the demo.
- **If you can't demo it, make a metaphor.** Sony Bravia's "Balls" and "Paint" dramatise "colour like no other".
- **Humour must *be* the point:** the ASUS rapping husband is the punchline to the battery life. GoPro "Owl Dance-Off" was funny but vague, and rated 3.
- **Excitement is not persuasion.** LG "David Warner" scored exciting 1.0 but effective 3, because the visual was pointless.
- **Brand patterns:**
  - **Apple** sells the user's identity and the emotional outcome, with minimal copy. "Shot on iPhone" shows what it can do and lets you connect the dots.
  - **Samsung** sells feature superiority, often against Apple.
  - **Sony** owns colour through metaphor.
  - **GoPro** sells the verb (capturing moments), not the noun.

## Rules

1. **Start with "because…":** one simple benefit (Cingular: "text all you want without paying extra").
2. **Demonstrate, always;** tech is abstract.
3. **If you can't demo, find a metaphor.**
4. **The joke must reveal the proposition.** If you can lift the joke out and the ad still works, the joke is a parasite.
5. **Find an enemy:** a competitor, a problem or an old way (Verizon "iDon't").
6. **Turn features into human benefits:** Havells sells "your daughter is safe", not "flame-retardant wire".
7. **Copy clarifies and never competes:** "It keeps going and going…" (Energizer). Less is more.
8. **Write the tagline first:** "Colour like no other", "Think different".
9. **Keep a confident tone.** Weak ads feel hesitant.

## Craft

- **The Apple-style hero reveal:** the product **stays still and the light moves**. A light sweep or fill fades in to reveal the form, with a 45° reflection line on screens and edge lighting along the lines. This is ideal for AI: static subject, moving light.
- **Macro details:** textures, ports, the camera module, buttons. Then **slow orbits or arcs with parallax** on a clean background.
- **Show real outcomes** (the photo it took, the battery at 38 days) as an **edit-composited screen insert.** Veo garbles UI text; our pipeline already supports real screen inserts.
- **Sound:** clean, precise foley (clicks, a soft whoosh on a reveal, the haptic tick), with a minimal, modern score.
- **Faceless fits naturally:** product only, or hands.
