Faceless product film in a bright, airy, high-key world. Everything is light: an all-white or pale set, soft diffused daylight, faint pale shadows, natural true-to-life colour with no warm or teal grade. Never dark, moody or slate.

Structure:
1. The problem, felt in one quiet image (the texture or moment the product fixes), shot in slow motion.
2. The product arrives: hands or a clean surface, the product used for real (pump, pour, open), slow motion, label facing camera and readable.
3. One shot per key ingredient or feature, each a real macro of the physical thing (a golden oil drop landing in a pool, a cream swirl, foam forming) in high-frame-rate slow motion. Real liquid physics only, never morphing or magical effects.
4. The result, shown on the real subject (hair, skin, surface) in a slow, graceful move.
5. Product hero: the pack upright and centred, a slow push-in, clean space left for the logo and tagline. A liquid hero moment (the product rising from a still pool of its own liquid) suits the first or last beat.

Camera: macro and close-ups on prime lenses, shallow depth of field, one slow motivated move per shot (push-in, slide, rise). No handheld shake.
Edit: soft dissolves of about 0.7s between every shot, no hard cuts, no speed-ups. Each feature gets a short two-line super in the empty side of its frame.
Sound: a light, airy music bed (piano or soft strings) with a soft voice-over that names each feature as it appears; gentle liquid sound accents.
Avoid: people's faces, competitor-like packs, any text painted into the footage, smoke, sparkles, lens flares.
