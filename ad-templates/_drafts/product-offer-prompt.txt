Create a premium, soft and elegant vertical product-offer poster template for [Product], reusable for skincare, personal care, baby care, beauty, wellness, home care, and everyday consumer products running a discount or special offer.

FORMAT:
Portrait vertical layout optimized for Instagram, WhatsApp, mobile ads, and digital campaigns. Keep a clear visual flow:
[Brand Logo] → [Main Headline] → [Secondary Headline] → [Lifestyle / Product Visual] → [Offer] → [Supporting Message] → [Call to Action].

MAIN VISUAL:
Make the product the unmistakable hero, shown clearly in the foreground or lower-middle area with clean, premium packaging. Pair it with one natural lifestyle subject that fits [Product] — for example a happy child for baby care, glowing skin for skincare, a calm home for home care — with a genuine, unposed expression and natural anatomy. Use soft commercial photography with a warm, caring, trustworthy atmosphere.

BRANDING:
Place [Brand Logo] small and clear near the top. Preserve any supplied logo exactly. Never invent logos, certifications, awards, ingredients, medical claims, statistics, or contact details.

HEADLINE:
Make [Main Headline] large, elegant, and highly readable in the upper section, using a soft, sophisticated display typeface. Place [Secondary Headline] beneath it as a quieter line in a clean sans-serif.

OFFER:
Show [Offer] exactly as supplied inside a distinctive rounded or organic premium badge near the product. Make it immediately noticeable without overpowering the product or headline. Never invent discounts or prices.

SUPPORTING MESSAGE:
Set [Supporting Message] as one short, calm line in a clean area with generous space around it.

CALL TO ACTION:
Place [Call to Action] near the bottom in a refined pill-shaped button.

COLOR:
Adapt a controlled, gentle palette to [Brand Colors] and [Product] — creamy ivory, soft pastels, light beige, and subtle warm neutrals by default. Avoid rainbow or harsh saturated colors.

BACKGROUND:
A soft premium background with subtle creamy gradients, gentle abstract shapes, or minimal product-inspired details (bubbles, petals, fabric, water) that stay quiet behind the text.

LIGHTING:
Soft diffused commercial light, gentle highlights, realistic shadows, natural skin tones, and polished product illumination.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Two complementary fonts with a clear hierarchy: headline, secondary headline, offer, supporting message, CTA. Every word crisp, correctly spelled, well kerned, and readable on mobile.

ADAPTABILITY:
Change the product, lifestyle subject, colors, background, and mood to suit the supplied product while keeping the same soft, premium, editorial design system.

NEGATIVE:
No copied artwork, fake branding, invented claims, random letters, extra text, distorted people, malformed hands, warped products, clutter, harsh colors, heavy shadows, excessive glow, or obvious AI artifacts.

Final result: a finished, professionally art-directed product-offer poster — soft, premium, trustworthy, and instantly clear about the product, the offer, and the CTA.
