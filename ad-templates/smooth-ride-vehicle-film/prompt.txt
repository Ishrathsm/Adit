Cinematic vehicle launch film, feature-led and calm. Natural true-to-life colour, golden-hour or soft morning light, real locations (coastal road, open highway, city edge at dusk).

Structure:
1. Wake-up detail: the headlight or display comes on in a dark frame, extreme close-up, slow.
2. Engineering details, one per shot: engine, brakes, a design line, each lit by a slow light sweep on a still vehicle, with the brand badge visible on the body.
3. Roll-out: the vehicle eases out of a garage or shade into light, steady and quiet.
4. The ride: a smooth, steady ride at a calm speed, camera tracking alongside at the same speed (vehicle and background move together, no swapping backgrounds), a slight natural lean at most.
5. Hero: the vehicle parked at golden hour, slow arc or push-in, empty sky or road space for the logo, tagline and call to action.

Camera: long lenses for detail, a smooth tracking vehicle for the ride, one motivated move per shot, no shaky or whip moves.
Edit: shots held long enough to breathe (3 to 7 seconds), cut on motion, short dissolves only into and out of the ride. Feature callouts as two lines (feature name plus one spec) placed in empty frame space.
Sound: music-led mix, a modern, confident score that builds into the ride; engine and road sound low under the music.
Avoid: stunts, wheelies, deep leans, burnouts, smoke, sparks, lens flares, crowds, dramatic lighting changes, any text painted into the footage.
