Aspirational education promo following one student. Bright, natural daylight in a real home study space and a real campus; natural colour, no heavy grade. One student (the age of the audience), the same face, hair and clothes in every shot.

Structure:
1. Hook: the student at a desk with books, the moment before starting, slightly unsure.
2. The product moment: the student opens the laptop or app and leans in; the screen stays angled away from camera.
3. Benefits, one per shot, each shown through a real action: writing confidently in a notebook (close-up of hands), smiling at the screen as a doubt is answered, a parent looking in proudly over the shoulder.
4. The payoff: the student walking across a campus or into a new place with quiet confidence, a slow push-in to a calm, proud look to camera.
5. End card with the course name, the offer and the sign-up line.

Camera: medium and close shots on prime lenses, soft natural light, gentle dolly moves, faces in sharp focus.
Edit: steady rhythm of about 3 to 4 seconds a shot, clean cuts on action, one short on-screen benefit line per shot from the second shot on, placed in the calm lower third.
Sound: an uplifting, light acoustic or piano score with a gentle build; no voice-over unless asked.
Avoid: exam stress drama, crying, crowds, readable screens, any text painted into the footage, logos of other schools.
