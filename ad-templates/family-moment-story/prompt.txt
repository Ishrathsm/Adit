A warm, real-life story told like a short film, set in a lived-in home (a real kitchen, a doorway, a living room with plants). Natural colour, soft window light, prime-lens look with shallow focus, an optional 2.39 letterbox. No humour unless asked; sincere and gentle.

Structure:
1. The small problem: one person, a relatable everyday discomfort (tired, cold, rushed), shown in a close-up of their face and posture.
2. The caring gesture: someone close (a neighbour, parent or friend) notices and helps; shot over-the-shoulder and through door frames like a film, never flat and frontal.
3. The product made or served for real (a cup poured, a dish plated), with the pack visible beside it.
4. The relief: a slow, quiet close-up as they take a sip or bite and visibly ease, a small genuine smile.
5. Pack hero: the pack and the served product on a warm surface, gentle steam or a slow push-in, space for the logo and tagline.

Camera: one motivated move per shot, cut on action, everyone in the same wardrobe across shots.
Edit: unhurried but never slow, no speed changes; J- and L-cuts, room tone under every cut, music fades of at least a second.
Sound: a light, happy score on one traditional instrument; a short warm voice-over carries the story; the brand name is spoken only by the voice-over, never by the people on screen.
Avoid: medical or health claims, comedy, exaggerated reactions, any text painted into the footage.
